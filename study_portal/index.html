Your 5-Day Systematic Exam Plan

Study ONE unit from EACH subject every day. This distributed learning model prevents cognitive fatigue and builds long-term memory across all four subjects simultaneously.

📅 Recommended Daily Study Cycle (Approx. 4.5 – 5 Hours / Day):
  1. Morning Block 1 (60 min): BCA-5004 (Numerical Methods) — Solve 2 worked numericals on paper.
  2. Break (15 min): Rest eyes, hydrate.
  3. Midday Block 2 (60 min): BCA-5003 (Computer Networks) — Understand layer mechanisms and protocols.
  4. Afternoon Block 3 (60 min): BCA-5002 (Java Programming) — Study syntax, lifecycle diagrams & code examples.
  5. Evening Block 4 (50 min): BCA-5001 (Knowledge Management) — Grasp real-world business & KM models.
  6. Night Finish (30 min): Complete the 10-Question MCQ Mastery Quiz at the end of each studied unit!
